Code, Campus & Capstone

Looking back at my Information Technology experience, projects, and milestones at George Mason University.

Transferring to George Mason University hasn’t just given me a career path, it has given me so much to learn and experience. It has honestly been an eye-opening chapter where I’ve pushed past what I thought I was capable of. When I first picked Information Technology, I barely knew anything about computers or coding, I mostly chose it out of peer pressure, never expecting it to become a real passion. Once I started taking classes at Mason, I saw how huge the potential of IT really is, and I fell in love with what I was learning and building on my own. Add in the campus buildings, the atmosphere, and the people I’ve met along the way, and the whole experience has just been amazing.
